👤 User Journey: Book a Facility

  1. 👋 User opens GameArena app
  2. 🔍 Browses facilities or applies filter (optional)
  3. 👆 Taps on desired facility (e.g., Cricket Ground A)
  4. 📅 Selects date from next 7 days
  5. ⏰ Chooses available time slot (turns blue)
  6. 💰 Reviews price ($50.00) on "Book Now" button
  7. ✅ Confirms booking by tapping button
  8. 🏠 Automatically returns to home screen
  9. 📜 Optionally views booking in history tab
⏱️ Total Time: 60-90 seconds
📱 Screens: 3 (Home → Detail → Home)
👆 Interactions: 6 taps
